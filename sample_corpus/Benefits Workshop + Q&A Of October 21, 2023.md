# Benefits Workshop + Q&A Of October 21, 2023

*Sample data: a fictional workshop transcript for a made-up company, written for this repository.*

Q: Do you still handle the expense claim the way the handbook says?

A: I've changed the expense claim. Don't email it any more: submit it in the expense tool, because emailed claims get lost. The amounts are the same as before. Some of you call it the expense report now, and that is fine, it is the same claim.

Q: And the hotel limit? The handbook says 180 dollars.

A: The standard limit stays 180 dollars per night. In the cities on the high-cost list you can go up to 250 dollars per night, but only with approval from a director. Never go above 250 dollars per night.

Q: How many days a week can I work remotely? In 2021 you said 2.

A: I allow a little more now. Remote days can be up to 3 days a week. Above 3 days you need written approval from a director.

Q: Last year you told one of us to skip the travel request. Should we all do that?

A: No. For her, because she had to fly out the same day for an urgent customer repair, I let her send the request after the trip. That was for her case. Everyone else sends the request first.

Q: Do part-time employees get the same vacation?

A: No. Part-time employees get vacation days in proportion to the hours they work. Contractors have no vacation days; their contract sets their days off.

Q: Is it still 2 weeks ahead for vacation?

A: Yes, with one exception: for vacation in December, ask 4 weeks ahead, because so many people ask at once.
