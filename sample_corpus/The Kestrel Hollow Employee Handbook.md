# The Kestrel Hollow Employee Handbook

*Sample data: a fictional employee handbook for a made-up company, written for this repository. First printed 2021.*

##### Expense Claim

For one business trip.

**1 approved TRAVEL REQUEST**
**180 dollars per night for the hotel**
**60 dollars per day for meals**
**1 receipt for every item over 25 dollars**

Fill in the claim in the expense tool and attach the receipts. Send it to finance within 30 days of the trip. Finance pays it within 10 days after your manager approves it.

##### Travel Request

Fill in the request in the expense tool at least 10 days before you book anything. Give the dates, the city and the estimated cost. Your manager approves it. Keep the approval, because the claim is paid only with it attached. An approval is valid for 90 days.

##### Direct Deposit

Open the expense tool, choose Profile and enter your bank details. HR checks them. The setup is active after two weeks. Until then, payments still come by cheque. Change your bank details the same way.

##### Vacation

Full-time employees get 20 vacation days per year, from the first day of work. Up to 5 unused days carry over into the next year and must be used by March 31. Ask for vacation at least 2 weeks ahead.

##### Sick Leave

Every employee gets 10 paid sick days per year. Tell your manager on the first morning.

##### Remote Work

Employees may work remotely up to 2 days a week. Agree on fixed days with your manager. Core hours are 10 to 3.

##### Storing Records

Keep receipts as photos for one year. Keep approvals in your email until the claim is paid.
