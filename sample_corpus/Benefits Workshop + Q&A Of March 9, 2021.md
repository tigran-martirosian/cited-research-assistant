# Benefits Workshop + Q&A Of March 9, 2021

*Sample data: a fictional workshop transcript for a made-up company, written for this repository.*

Q: Why do I need a travel request at all?

A: Because finance cannot pay for a trip it did not know about. The approved request is what your claim is checked against. Without it the claim is rejected, even for a small trip. Send the request at least 10 days before you book anything.

Q: Do I have to keep my receipts?

A: Yes. Keep a receipt for every item over 25 dollars. A photo is fine. An item over 25 dollars without a receipt is not paid.

Q: How do I file the expense claim?

A: The way it is in the handbook, where I still called it the Expense Claim. For one trip: an approved travel request, up to 180 dollars per night for the hotel, up to 60 dollars per day for meals, and a receipt for every item over 25 dollars. Email the claim to finance within 30 days of the trip. Finance pays it within 10 days after your manager approves it.

Q: Can I carry over all my unused vacation days?

A: No. Never carry over more than 5 days. Anything above 5 is lost on December 31. The days you carry over must be used by March 31.

Q: How many days a week can I work remotely?

A: For remote work, 2 days a week at most. Agree on fixed days with your manager. Core hours are 10 to 3 on every day, in the office or not.

Q: Someone told me a colleague works from her parents' country for a month.

A: That is working abroad, and it is a different thing from remote days. Working abroad is up to 15 working days a year and needs approval from HR. Do not mix the two: the limit for remote days does not apply to working abroad.

Q: Is there anything that gets the money back sooner?

A: Direct deposit. Direct deposit would probably make the payout about twice as fast. Without it, finance sends a cheque by post. You set it up in the expense tool, and I explain the steps in the handbook.

Q: How far ahead do I ask for vacation?

A: At least 2 weeks ahead for a week or more. For a single day, 3 days ahead is enough.
