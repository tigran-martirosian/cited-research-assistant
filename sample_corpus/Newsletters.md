# Newsletters

*Sample data: fictional newsletter items for a made-up company, written for this repository.*

## March 2022

Several of you asked whether sick days use up your vacation days. Sick days do not reduce your vacation the way unpaid leave does. What reduces vacation days is unpaid leave, not sick leave.

A reminder about the cheque: if you have not set up direct deposit, the cheque goes to the address in your file. Check that it is current.

## October 2022

Photos of receipts must show the whole receipt, with the date and the total. A cropped photo is rejected, and so is a photo of several receipts together.

For one field-service team that travels at short notice, I allow meals up to 80 dollars a day. That is for that team. For everyone else, the handbook limit stays.

## June 2023

Sick days beyond what the allowance covers still come out of your vacation. If you are sick for more than 10 days in a year, the extra days are taken from your vacation days. I still stand by what I wrote in 2022, but it has this limit.
